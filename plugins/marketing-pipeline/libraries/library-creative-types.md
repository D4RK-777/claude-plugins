# Library: Creative Types
> The definitive taxonomy of ad creative styles for paid social. Every creative produced in this pipeline should be tagged with a Format + Style combination. This drives deliberate creative strategy rather than guesswork.

---

## HOW TO USE THIS LIBRARY

Every ad creative = **Format** × **Style** × **Hook Structure**

- **Format** = the container (static image, video length, carousel)
- **Style** = the visual/narrative approach (what the viewer experiences)
- **Hook Structure** = the opening mechanism (see `library-hook-structures.md`)

When briefing creative, always specify all three. Example:
> "Short-form video (15s) / UGC Talking Head / Problem Agitation Hook"

---

## PART 1: FORMATS

### Static Image
- **Best for:** Quick awareness, retargeting, offer announcements, product launches
- **Aspect ratios:** 1:1 (square), 4:5 (portrait feed), 9:16 (story/reel cover)
- **Performance note:** Drives ~60–70% of Meta conversions. Fastest to test. Lower CPM than video.
- **When to use:** Testing new angles cheaply, high-frequency retargeting, simple offers

### Short-Form Video (0–15s)
- **Best for:** Top-of-funnel attention, Reels/TikTok/Stories placement
- **Aspect ratio:** 9:16 vertical
- **Performance note:** Reels delivers 26% lower CPC than Feed. Sound-on assumed.
- **When to use:** Pattern interrupts, UGC, before/after reveals, hooks that need motion

### Mid-Form Video (15–60s)
- **Best for:** Explanation, demonstration, problem-agitation-solution narratives
- **Aspect ratio:** 9:16 or 4:5
- **Performance note:** Enough time to build emotional arc. Strong for cold audiences.
- **When to use:** Founder stories, product demos, testimonials, complex products

### Long-Form Video (60s+)
- **Best for:** Deep storytelling, brand awareness, YouTube pre-roll, warm retargeting
- **When to use:** High-consideration products, webinar teasers, case study formats

### Carousel (2–10 cards)
- **Best for:** Multiple products, sequential storytelling, step-by-step education
- **Mechanic:** Each card is a hook in itself — card 1 must stop the scroll
- **Performance note:** Higher engagement rate than single image. Good for bottom-of-funnel.
- **When to use:** Product range showcase, comparison charts, tip listicles, before/after

### Collection
- **Best for:** E-commerce, product catalogue, instant storefront
- **Mechanic:** Hero video/image + 4 product tiles below
- **When to use:** Catalogue-driven campaigns, seasonal sales, product discovery

### Dynamic / Catalogue
- **Best for:** Retargeting, abandoned cart, personalised product feed
- **When to use:** Warm audiences who've viewed products, e-commerce retargeting

## PART 2: CREATIVE STYLES (16 Types)

---

### 1. PRODUCT HERO
**What it is:** Clean, high-production product shot. The product is the star — minimal background, professional lighting, no person present.
**Visual cues:** White/minimal background, studio lighting, high-res, product-centred
**Best formats:** Static image, carousel
**Funnel stage:** Mid–Bottom
**Pair with hooks:** Bold Statement, Social Proof Number, Offer/Urgency
**Use when:** Audience already knows the category. You're selling the product, not the concept.
**Avoid when:** Cold audience — they don't care about the product yet

---

### 2. LIFESTYLE
**What it is:** Product shown in aspirational, real-world context. The person or setting sells the identity/feeling.
**Visual cues:** Natural light, real environments, person present but relaxed, product is contextual
**Best formats:** Static image, mid-form video
**Funnel stage:** Top–Mid
**Pair with hooks:** Transformation, Relatable Moment, Story
**Use when:** Identity/aspiration drives the purchase. The customer buys who they'll become.
**Avoid when:** Audience needs education — lifestyle doesn't explain what the product does

---

### 3. UGC (User-Generated Content Style)
**What it is:** Lo-fi, authentic-feeling content that mimics real user footage. Can be actual UGC or produced to look like it.
**Visual cues:** Handheld camera feel, natural/home lighting, casual setting, relatable person
**Best formats:** Short-form video, mid-form video
**Funnel stage:** Top–Mid
**Performance note:** 4× higher CTR than polished creative. Cuts CPC by up to 50%.
**Pair with hooks:** Testimonial, Problem Agitation, Relatable Moment, Story
**Avoid when:** High-luxury brand where polish = brand value

---

### 4. TALKING HEAD
**What it is:** A single person speaking directly to camera. Can be polished or lo-fi.
**Visual cues:** Person fills frame, direct eye contact with camera
**Best formats:** Short-form video, mid-form video
**Funnel stage:** Top–Mid
**Variants:** Founder (authenticity), Customer (social proof), Expert (authority)
**Pair with hooks:** Question, Problem Agitation, Curiosity Gap, Education

---

### 5. TESTIMONIAL
**What it is:** Real or scripted customer story. Customer voice does the selling.
**Visual cues:** Quote text overlay, star ratings, customer photo, specific outcome mentioned
**Best formats:** Static image (quote card), short-form video, carousel
**Funnel stage:** Mid–Bottom
**Performance note:** Meta reports 7.5% higher conversion when testimonial elements present.
**Formula:** Specific pain before + specific outcome after + timeframe = strong testimonial
**Pair with hooks:** Social Proof Number, Bold Statement, Story

---

### 6. BEFORE / AFTER
**What it is:** Direct visual or narrative split showing the transformation.
**Visual cues:** Split screen, sequential frames, left/right layout, time-stamp labels
**Best formats:** Static image (split), short-form video, carousel
**Funnel stage:** Top–Bottom (works across funnel)
**Variants:** Visual / Metric / Lifestyle / Emotional
**Pair with hooks:** Transformation Hook, Problem Agitation, Curiosity Gap

---

### 7. US VS THEM (COMPARISON)
**What it is:** Direct comparison between your product and competition (named or unnamed).
**Visual cues:** Two-column table, ✅ vs ❌ iconography, side-by-side comparison
**Best formats:** Static image, carousel, short-form video
**Funnel stage:** Mid–Bottom
**Variants:** Named competitor / Category comparison / Old way vs new way
**Pair with hooks:** Contrarian/Myth-Bust, Bold Statement, Problem Agitation
**Legal note:** Keep factual and verifiable. No false claims.

---

### 8. DEMO / SCREEN RECORDING
**What it is:** Shows the product being used. For software: screen recording. For physical: hands-on demo.
**Visual cues:** Screen capture overlay, hands interacting with product, zoom on key features
**Best formats:** Short-form video, mid-form video
**Funnel stage:** Mid–Bottom
**Pair with hooks:** Question, Curiosity Gap, Education, Bold Statement

---

### 9. FOUNDER STORY
**What it is:** The origin story. Why the brand exists. Who built it and why.
**Visual cues:** Behind-the-scenes, founder on camera, authentic/imperfect setting
**Best formats:** Mid-form video, long-form video
**Funnel stage:** Top–Mid
**Formula:** "I noticed [problem] → Everyone said [wrong solution] → I knew [insight] → So I built [product]"
**Pair with hooks:** Story Hook, Contrarian/Myth-Bust, Problem Agitation
**Avoid when:** Short-form placement — needs at least 60s to breathe

---

### 10. SOCIAL PROOF / PRESS
**What it is:** External validation. Reviews, ratings, media coverage, logos, user counts.
**Visual cues:** Star ratings, media logos, review counts, customer count, award badges
**Best formats:** Static image, carousel
**Funnel stage:** Mid–Bottom
**Variants:** Review aggregation / Press mention / Customer count / Case study thumbnail
**Pair with hooks:** Social Proof Number Hook, Bold Statement

---

### 11. PAIN / PROBLEM AGITATION
**What it is:** Opens on the problem state. Intensifies the pain before revealing the solution.
**Visual cues:** Frustrated person, failing metrics, chaotic/messy scenarios
**Best formats:** Short-form video, mid-form video, static image
**Funnel stage:** Top–Mid
**Formula:** Name it → Agitate it → Solve it (or let viewer complete the thought)
**Pair with hooks:** Problem Agitation Hook, Fear Hook, Relatable Moment

---

### 12. EDUCATION / VALUE-FIRST
**What it is:** Leads with genuine value. Teaches something useful. Product is the vehicle, not the hero.
**Visual cues:** Numbered tips, step-by-step visuals, clean text overlays, swipe prompts
**Best formats:** Carousel (most effective), short-form video, mid-form video
**Funnel stage:** Top–Mid
**Pair with hooks:** Education Hook, Curiosity Gap, Question
**Note:** Must be genuinely useful. Watered-down tips erode trust.

---

### 13. MEME / CULTURAL
**What it is:** Taps into existing cultural moments, meme formats, trending audio/visuals.
**Visual cues:** Recognisable meme templates, trending formats, cultural references
**Best formats:** Static image, short-form video
**Funnel stage:** Top
**Shelf life:** Short — cultural moments expire fast
**Risk:** Trying too hard reads as cringe. The brand must belong in the moment.
**Pair with hooks:** Pattern Interrupt, Relatable Moment, Contrarian

---

### 14. UNBOXING / REVEAL
**What it is:** The product reveal experience. Taps into gift psychology.
**Visual cues:** Packaging, hands opening product, reveal moment, reaction
**Best formats:** Short-form video, mid-form video
**Funnel stage:** Top–Mid
**Pair with hooks:** Curiosity Gap, Story, Pattern Interrupt

---

### 15. ANIMATION / MOTION GRAPHICS
**What it is:** Branded animated content — illustrated characters, kinetic text, explainer animations.
**Visual cues:** Illustration style, smooth motion, brand colours dominant, no live footage
**Best formats:** Short-form video, mid-form video
**Funnel stage:** Mid (best for explaining rather than interrupting)
**Pair with hooks:** Education, Bold Statement, Curiosity Gap
**Note:** Higher production cost but infinitely scalable.

---

### 16. PATTERN INTERRUPT
**What it is:** Anything unexpected enough to stop the scroll before the hook even lands.
**Visual cues:** Unexpected first frame, deliberate "wrongness", jarring contrast
**Best formats:** Short-form video (first frame critical), static image
**Funnel stage:** Top
**Pair with hooks:** Pattern Interrupt Hook, Curiosity Gap, Contrarian

## PART 3: FUNNEL MAPPING

| Funnel Stage | Primary Styles | Secondary Styles |
|---|---|---|
| Cold / Awareness | UGC, Pain-Problem, Pattern Interrupt, Meme | Lifestyle, Education, Founder Story |
| Consideration | Demo, Before-After, Us-vs-Them, Talking Head | Testimonial, Education, Founder Story |
| Retargeting / Conversion | Testimonial, Social Proof, Product Hero | Before-After, Offer/Urgency, Comparison |

---

## PART 4: CREATIVE MIX STRATEGY

**70/20/10 Budget Rule:**
| % | Creative | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| 70% | Proven winners | Revenue generation |
| 20% | Iterations of winners (new hook, same style) | Optimisation |
| 10% | Experimental (new style, new angle) | Innovation |

**Testing Rules:**
1. Change only ONE variable at a time (style, hook, format — never two at once)
2. Know format winner before testing style
3. Minimum 3 styles per campaign
4. Retire at 30% CTR drop — rotate before quality score damage
